How buying works

  1. Write to hello@legatia.solutions with your company's name and country, and what you will run omnigate for.
  2. You get an invoice and the license terms by email. Nothing is owed until you have read them and agreed.
  3. You pay the invoice in USDC on Solana, with the payment link on it. If that does not work for your company, say so in your email.
  4. You get the confirmation of your license for the year, by email. There is nothing to install or activate.

Questions

Do I need a license to try it at work? Evaluating it is not what the price is for. Once it runs for your business, it needs the license.

Is anything different in the paid software? No. There is one omnigate. The license changes what you may do with it, not what it does.

What is the binding text? For noncommercial use, the LICENSE file. For commercial use, the terms you receive with the invoice. This page is a summary and changes neither.
